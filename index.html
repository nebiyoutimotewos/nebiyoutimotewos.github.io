About Me À propos de moi

Nebiyou Timotewos is a Canadian entrepreneur, humanitarian, bestselling author, and community leader recognized with more than 30 international leadership, innovation, and community service awards.

His honours include The Diana Award, the Robert J. Tiffin Leadership Award, and the Global Visionaries Educational Innovation Award, for which he was recognized as its youngest recipient and first Canadian.

He is the Founder and CEO of Occasio Technologies Inc., an AI-powered opportunity platform helping Canadian students discover scholarships, grants, internships, volunteer opportunities, and more through intelligent matching, personalized recommendations, and application tracking.

Built on the belief that opportunity should never depend on who you know or where you come from, Occasio aims to make opportunity discovery more accessible, personalized, and equitable.

Nebiyou began volunteering at 15 and has since founded and led several youth-focused initiatives. Through Brothers4Brothers, he has supported young men and challenged the stigma surrounding mental health. Through Communities Care, he has helped provide essential resources and support to more than 3,000 youth and families.

His journey, shaped by experiences in Ethiopia, displacement during the war in Yemen, and rebuilding his life after immigrating to Canada, continues to drive his commitment to removing barriers and creating pathways for others to succeed.

Grâce à Communities Care, il a contribué à fournir des ressources essentielles et du soutien à plus de 3 000 jeunes et familles.

Son parcours, façonné par ses expériences en Éthiopie, son déplacement pendant la guerre au Yémen et la reconstruction de sa vie après son immigration au Canada, continue de guider son engagement à éliminer les obstacles et à créer des voies vers la réussite pour les autres.
